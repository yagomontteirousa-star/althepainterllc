The person behind the work

Built from the work up.

Algernon Scott has spent more than 25 years learning how a surface behaves, how a color changes a room, and when the smallest detail is the one that matters most.

A Virginia native, Al moved to Marlborough, Massachusetts, at 18 and entered the painting trade just two years later. The discipline he developed on New England homes and commercial properties became the foundation of Al The Painter LLC.

Today, Al remains closely involved in the work. The Sterling shop supports cabinet and millwork refinishing, spray-booth application, and careful material storage—giving specialized finishes the controlled environment they require.
