# Al The Painter LLC — compact brand guide

## Brand idea

Experienced, composed, and hands-on. The visual language should feel more like a well-made editorial portfolio than a generic contractor template.

## Palette

| Role | Color |
| --- | --- |
| ATP brand blue | `#011971` |
| Ink background | `#09121D` |
| Soft ink | `#0E1A28` |
| Workshop panel | `#142437` |
| Cool steel | `#C7CCD2` |
| Soft white | `#F2F3F3` |

Do not introduce safety yellow, warm beige, purple gradients, or glow effects. Use the official blue for the main conversion area, deep ink for the dominant canvas, and cool steel as a controlled tonal break.

## Typography

- Display: **Newsreader**, weights 400–600
- Body and UI: **Manrope**, weights 400–700

Keep headings in mixed case with short line lengths. Reserve uppercase for navigation, labels, and compact supporting data.

## Composition

Use photography at meaningful scale, one-pixel rules, asymmetrical project layouts, and generous negative space. Avoid repeated cards, oversized badges, heavy shadows, and decorative spray trails.
